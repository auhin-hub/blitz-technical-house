02 System Parameters
Radiator Geometry
Louver/Wave Pitch — Ismael et al. (2016) show the fine corrugation pitch inside each fin (distinct from Fin Pitch above) has a strong, independently-measured effect on heat rejection.
When enabled, h_air is scaled by (wave pitch / 2.5)-0.63 — an empirical exponent curve-fit (R²=0.89) from Tables 1–5 of Ismael, Yun & Ulugbek (2016), Radiator Heat Dissipation Performance, J. Electronics Cooling & Thermal Control 6, 88–96. Reference case: P = 2.5 mm.
Operating Conditions
Max Safe Coolant Temp — the solver upper bound and pass/fail threshold. KTM 390 limit = 110 °C.
Drivetrain — Speed from RPM
Vehicle speed is calculated from RPM, gear ratios, and tyre diameter. Analysis runs across all gears at every RPM point.
Fan Model
On/Off thermostat control — fan is OFF below trigger temperature, ON at full CFM above it. This is the stock KTM behaviour.
Fan state is solved automatically: if the no-fan steady-state temperature exceeds the trigger, the fan switches on and the system re-solves. Fan contributes to cooling only when actually needed.
Pump Curve — Flow vs RPM
Enter RPM and flow rate pairs — the pump flow at each engine RPM. Default is the standard KTM 390 estimate. Edit or add rows for other engines.
